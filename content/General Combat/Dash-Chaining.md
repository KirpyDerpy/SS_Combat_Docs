[[Dash-Chaining]] is a method of canceling the upward momentum from landing a dash attack into another attack. This can be used to either rack up damage very fast or get in a quick hit before retreating.

# Prerequisites
- ![[swift_step_dash_ancestral_art_hollow_knight_silksong_wiki_guide_300px.png|30]][[Swift Step]]
# Instructions
[[Dash-Chaining|Dash-Chains]] are pretty simple:


| *1.* Start Dashing        *2.* Dash-attack       *3.* Right before the attack lands, buffer another dash |
| :------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------: |
|                         ![[Hollow Knight- Silksong - 2025-09-28 7-04-27 PM.mp4]]                         |

When put together, this can be used to string multiple attacks dealing massive damage in a short time

Full speed demonstration:
![[Hollow Knight- Silksong - 2025-09-28 7-05-58 PM.mp4]]
# Use Cases
- [[Widow]]
	- Similarly to [[Bounce-Chaining]], [[Dash-Chaining]] can be used in moments to get off a little bit of extra damage during sweeping attacks

# Limitations
- Can only be used on the ground
- Shaman Crest almost entirely blocks the usage
- Beast Crest can't chain more than 1 attack due to its jumping dash-attack
- Wanderer Crest can only chain after its secondary dash-attack
- Reaper Crest is unable to preform this tech
